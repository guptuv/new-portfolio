A mechanical engineer who fell for code — by way of rovers, drones, and a lot of SLAM.

My robotics work spans the full stack: mechanical design (a 3D-printed triple-bogie rover), perception (visual-inertial SLAM, RTAB-Map point clouds, RealSense detection), control (nonlinear and bio-inspired controllers) and simulation (Gazebo, PX4 SITL, Unity forest worlds).

I studied Mechanical Engineering at VNIT Nagpur, but spent most of my time in labs: building a six-wheeled rover for ISRO's robotics challenge, writing visual-inertial SLAM pipelines for UAVs at IIIT Hyderabad, and simulating forest-crawling rovers for the University of Southampton.

Today I'm a Software Engineer at Ether, working on ether-be — the backend of a school-management platform — across schemas, services, APIs and the transformer layers that feed the frontend. Same instinct as robotics: make systems that are robust when the terrain gets rough.
